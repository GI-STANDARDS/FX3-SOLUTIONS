# Code of Conduct

> Community standards for issues, pull requests, and discussions in FX3-SOLUTIONS.

## 1. Scope

This code applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, commit discussions, and any other communication connected to the repository. It applies to maintainers and contributors equally.

## 2. Expected behaviour

- Be technically honest. State what you measured, what you inferred, and what you do not know.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Assume good faith until the evidence says otherwise.
- Give enough context for someone else to reproduce your result.
- Respect licensing boundaries and third-party ownership.
- Accept correction when the evidence is better than yours.

## 3. Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on identity.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without consent, including names, addresses, account identifiers, or private file paths.
- Spam, repeated off-topic posts, or deliberate thread derailment.
- Encouraging piracy, DRM circumvention, or redistribution of proprietary game assets.
- Posting game archives, extracted assets, or third-party material you do not have the right to share.

## 4. Modding-specific rules

Far Cry 3 modding touches proprietary material. In this project:

- Do not request or share pirated copies of the game or editor.
- Do not request or share cracks, DRM bypasses, or license-key workarounds.
- Do not upload game archives, extracted `.xbt`/`.dds` files, or `.fc3map` files.
- Do not redistribute third-party object packs. Link to the author's distribution instead.
- Do not present Ubisoft-derived material as your own work.

## 5. Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the GI-STANDARDS GitHub profile. If no private contact is available, open an issue asking the maintainer to make contact, without including the details. If the concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's report-abuse flow.

Include what happened, where, when, and any relevant links or screenshots. Do not publish the report publicly.

## 6. Enforcement

The maintainer may take any action proportionate to the conduct, including:

1. A private correction or warning.
2. Editing or removing a comment, issue, or pull request.
3. Locking a thread.
4. A temporary ban from project spaces.
5. A permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and are not subject to a formal appeal process. A person who reports a concern in good faith will not be penalised for the report.

## 7. Good-faith corrections

Technical disagreement is not misconduct. If someone corrects a measurement, a path, or a claim with evidence, treat it as a contribution. The project's purpose is to replace guesswork with measured facts.
